Hey, I'm Geri. I link your Discord account to Chess.com and show stats, last games, and a server leaderboard.

Commands (prefer **/** slash; **$** prefix works too):
• **addcdc** — save your Chess.com username (optional real name)
• **cdcprofile** — your ratings and profile link (optional: another member's Discord name if they're registered)
• **lastcdc** — your most recent Chess.com game + GIF (optional: a Chess.com username)
• **cdcleaderboard** — ratings for everyone registered here (optional: bullet, blitz, rapid, …)
